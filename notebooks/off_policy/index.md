# Off-Policy Examples

Off-policy algorithms store transitions in a replay buffer and can learn from past experience.

- [DQN on CartPole-v1](dqn_cartpole.ipynb) — Deep Q-Network (discrete actions)
- [SAC on LunarLanderContinuous-v2](sac_lunarlander.ipynb) — Soft Actor-Critic (continuous actions)
- [DDPG on LunarLanderContinuous-v2](ddpg_lunarlander.ipynb) — Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient
- [TD3 on LunarLanderContinuous-v2](td3_lunarlander.ipynb) — Twin Delayed DDPG
